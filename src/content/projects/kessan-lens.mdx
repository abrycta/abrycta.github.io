## Problem

Japanese companies file their annual and quarterly securities reports with the
Financial Services Agency's EDINET system as XBRL. The data is public and free,
but it is organised by filing rather than by company, and account titles are in
Japanese. Comparing five years of a company's statements, or two peers, in
English takes real effort.

## Approach

- **Ingest:** a nightly Java ETL pulls new filings from the EDINET API v2
  (incremental pulls only; EDINET does not allow bulk downloads).
- **Normalise:** account titles are mapped to English with the JP–EN account-title
  lists published by the exchange, into one tidy fact table. Company-specific
  elements without an English label keep their Japanese name, marked as such.
- **Serve:** a small API on Cloudflare Workers over a Turso database; the React
  front end shows five-year statements in Japanese or English, ratios and peer
  comparisons, with CSV downloads that carry the source attribution.
- **Test:** JUnit and Mockito for the ETL, with a real database container in CI.
