## Problem

Official poverty estimates for the Philippines' 1,600+ cities and municipalities
come from the Philippine Statistics Authority's small-area estimates, released
every few years (2012, 2015, 2018, 2021). Between releases, and after the latest
one, there is no municipal number. Most earlier satellite-based work for the
country is cross-sectional: one map for one year.

## Approach

- **Features:** yearly zonal statistics per municipality from VIIRS night-time
  lights (2012–2024) and MODIS vegetation indices (NDVI), computed in Google Earth Engine.
- **Labels:** the PSA small-area estimates, which publish a standard error and
  confidence interval per municipality, joined on the official geographic code.
- **Model:** a recurrent neural network (GRU/LSTM) following the design in my
  published research{/* TODO: link the paper once the page is ready */}, checked with
  leave-years-out backtests against a gradient-boosting baseline.
- **In the browser:** the trained model is exported to TensorFlow.js, so a
  "what if night-time radiance grew faster" slider recomputes the nowcast instantly.
- **Uncertainty first:** official estimates are shown with their intervals next to
  the model line and its backtest error. The few labelled years per municipality and
  the 2018 methodology change are stated, not hidden.
